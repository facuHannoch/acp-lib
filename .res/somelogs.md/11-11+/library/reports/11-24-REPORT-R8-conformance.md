## Existing journal compatibility

R7 never transmitted reminder changes to the real harness. A legacy
`maybe_sent` entry still carries the uncertainty represented by that state:
keep its logical identity and freeze the 0031 encoding before the first R8
attempt. Recover a legacy coalesced schedule anchor only where the journal
provides reliable evidence. An expiry value may have been clamped, so
inverting it cannot reliably recover the original lead. An ambiguous
legacy entry stays visible for explicit correction, with a diagnostic,
rather than sending a fabricated acceptance anchor or looping on its wake.
The cache's authoritative expiry and the saved schedule anchor are distinct
from the journal's retry deadline.

## Implementation and verification boundaries

Put protocol shapes and ISO conversion in one adapter. Keep journal transitions
and cache changes in repository scopes, release consumer events after commit,
and keep timer ownership outside the persistence transaction. Stage wire and
reconcile first, cold wake second, idle stop third. Use a scripted peer for
ordering, exact replay, conflict/stale/blocks, negotiated capabilities, paging
and expiry/dedupe. Use injected timers for early wakes, clock jumps, close and
manual-start races, overdue recovery and exhausted budgets. Exercise idle
refusal reasons, inclusive deadline boundary, and stop racing admission.
Real stdio acceptance waits for R-H2.
